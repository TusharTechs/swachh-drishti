From orbit to evidence, in five steps

  1. Find every site

    Climate TRACE, OpenStreetMap and the MoHUA DRAP list merged into one open gazetteer, with industrial dumps filtered out.

  2. Trace the mound

    Copernicus elevation finds the mound; Sentinel-2 draws its edge where no map exists.

  3. Watch it change

    A dry-season Sentinel-2 image for every year since 2019: exposed waste, greening, water.

  4. Fire & radar

    NASA VIIRS fire detections since 2019 and Sentinel-1 radar measuring whether the mound is settling.

  5. Reviewed evidence

    Agents draft a one-page pack per site; a reviewer gate blocks unsupported numbers, blame and names.
