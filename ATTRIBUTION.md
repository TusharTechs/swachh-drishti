# Data attribution

- **OpenStreetMap** contributors: landfill boundaries, geocoding (Nominatim). ODbL 1.0. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- **Climate TRACE**: solid-waste-disposal asset locations and emissions estimates. CC BY 4.0. https://climatetrace.org
- **Copernicus Sentinel-2** L2A (ESA/EU), accessed via Element 84 Earth Search on AWS: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data.
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-30**: © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the EU and ESA.
- **NASA FIRMS** VIIRS 375 m active fire data (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21). https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov
- **GHSL** GHS-POP R2023A (European Commission, Joint Research Centre). CC BY 4.0.
- **MoHUA / PIB**: DRAP concept note (Nov 2025) and Lakshya Zero Dumpsite backgrounder (Jan 2026), public documents.
- **Basemaps**: OpenFreeMap (OpenMapTiles, OSM); Sentinel-2 cloudless 2023 by EOX IT Services GmbH (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- Documented fire events: compiled from public news reports, NGT orders and RTI replies; source URL per row in `data/raw/ground_truth_fires.csv`.
